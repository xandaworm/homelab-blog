I have been building a homelab since August of 2023, my Junior year of college. Initially my setup was just an old desktop I had lying around and installed Proxmox hypervisor on it. I did all the basic homelab tasks: provisioned VMs, learned how to use docker and containerize, and configuring automated backups.

I found my passion for homelabbing when I set-up Pi-Hole as an adblocker, as I realized my homelab can supplement my life and solve little inconveniences like ads. More, it also helped me to understand concepts from my college classes and labs better as I was implementing them in a way that was directly beneficial to my day-to-day.

I also became obsessed with the concept of digital sovereignty, so I did what everybody else does when they first get interested in digital sovereignty: Replace streaming services with their own Plex server. As I built my Plex, I realized I needed a storage solution, so I purchased a used Synology NAS off of eBay, and a couple of WD Red 8TB HDDs (Remember when they were affordable? I do!) and created a RAID1 array to store my media and backups.

Sadly, the motherboard in the old desktop died, so I was without a server for a while, and hosted any useful lightweight containers I had on my NAS instead. I purchased a used mini-PC after graduating college, and restored my Proxmox backup on it. Since then, I am always working on something new for my homelab and am more excited than ever to work on it!
